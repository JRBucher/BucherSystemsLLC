Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bucher Systems LLC ("Bucher Systems," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with HopWire, our software platform for breweries and small venues. HopWire is a product of Bucher Systems LLC. By using HopWire or connecting a Meta business account to it, you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

Bucher Systems LLC is an independent software company based in Virginia, USA. We operate HopWire, a platform that lets small and mid-sized businesses manage their events and specials in one place, including an optional integration with Meta (Facebook).

Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and business details you provide when you create a HopWire account.
  • Meta Platform Data: When a client connects their own Facebook Page to HopWire, we receive data from that Page through Meta, including the list of Pages the client manages and event and post information such as titles, images, dates, and descriptions.
  • Usage information: Basic technical and usage data, such as device type and app activity, used to operate and improve the service.

How we use information

We use the information above only to provide and improve the HopWire service. Specifically, we use Meta Platform Data to:

  • Let a client select which Facebook Page to connect to HopWire.
  • Display the client's events, releases, and specials inside HopWire.
  • Allow the client to create, edit, and manage that content.

Each client only has access to data from the Page it connected. We do not sell, rent, or trade Meta Platform Data or any personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

How we share information

We do not share your information except in these limited cases: with service providers who help us operate HopWire (for example, hosting) under confidentiality obligations; when required by law; or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and our business. We do not share Meta Platform Data with third parties for their own purposes.

Data retention

We retain Meta Platform Data only for as long as needed to provide the service. When a client disconnects their Facebook Page or deletes their HopWire account, we delete the associated Meta Platform Data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

How to request deletion of your data

You can request deletion of your data at any time by disconnecting your Facebook Page in HopWire, or by emailing us at contact@buchersystems.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request." We will delete the associated data within 30 days and confirm once it is done.

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard the data we hold.

Children's privacy

HopWire is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.
