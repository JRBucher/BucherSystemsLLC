HopWire — manage your venue's events in one place.

HopWire is a software platform by Bucher Systems LLC that helps breweries and small venues publish and manage their events, releases, and daily specials — and keep them in sync with their Facebook Page.

How it works

About Bucher Systems LLC

Bucher Systems LLC is an independent software company based in Virginia, USA. We build HopWire, a platform that small and mid-sized businesses use to manage their events and specials in one place, including an integration with Meta that lets them connect their own Facebook Page.

How the Facebook integration works

HopWire is a SaaS platform. Our clients — breweries and small venues — connect their own Meta business account and Facebook Page to HopWire so they can manage their events and specials without switching between tools.

1Connect your Page

A client signs in with Facebook Login for Business and grants HopWire access to the Facebook Page they manage.

2We sync your event data

HopWire reads the event and Page details from that connected Page — titles, images, dates, and descriptions — and displays them inside the app.

3Create, edit, and manage

Clients create, edit, and manage their events and specials in HopWire. Each client only sees data from the Page they connected.

What data we use and why

We only use the Platform Data needed to provide the service above. We do not sell client data, and each business only has access to the data from the Page it connected.

  • Facebook Page detailsThe list of Pages a client manages, so they can choose which Page to connect.
  • Event and post informationTitles, images, dates, and descriptions, used to display and manage events and specials inside HopWire.
